# Made product identities

The Plump, Time, Link and Carrel campaign assets, first published in October 2026.

For new products, follow the [Made publishing guide](../../../MADE.md) and [product brief](../../../templates/made-product.md). This file and the generation JSON records document the selected assets; the guide owns the repeatable workflow and release checks.

- Each product owns a geometric SVG. Products with `model: true` embed the same outline in their Plump `scene.json` (v1) and exported GLB. Carrel does not offer a 3D model. The parent zyx mark remains the site signature.
- Plump uses chrome and volume, Time uses amber glass and light, Link uses vermilion silicone and connection, and Carrel uses cobalt ceramic and a sheltered workspace.
- Material and campaign WebPs are AI art directed images generated with `google/gemini-3.1-flash-image` through OpenRouter. They interpret the supplied outlines; they are not screenshots of the renderer or photographs of actual products or customers.
- Desktop campaigns are 1920 × 1080. Mobile campaigns are separately composed 1080 × 1920 images.
- The live model viewer loads `@workspace/3d`, the same renderer as Plump. It loads lighting from Plump only after the viewer opens. Its lighting and geometry are distinct from the generated campaign images.

The geometric material identities were developed in the original study at <https://dev.zyx.tw/made-seeds/index.html>. The current photographs use contemporary Japanese and Korean fashion-editorial styling, adult female models, restrained white and gray clothing, natural expressions and one oversized product shape. The INTENTION [Talky Made page](https://www.intention.ltd/made?p=talky) informed the photographic direction; the generated scenes and model identities are original.

The original three campaigns were regenerated on October 2, 2026. Plump pairs chrome with an indoor doorway, Time pairs amber glass with a cool green laundromat, and Link pairs vermilion silicone with a pale urban wall. Each mobile composition preserves its desktop model, styling and prop, leaving space above for live page text. Desktop and mobile generation prompts are preserved in [plump-generation.json](./plump-generation.json), [time-generation.json](./time-generation.json) and [link-generation.json](./link-generation.json).

The approved Time photograph uses a distant view, a quiet waiting pose and an amber ring among circular washing-machine doors. All four campaigns receive the same deterministic film finish: downsampling, optical softness, highlight bloom, restrained saturation, lifted blacks and seeded luminance grain. The original photographs stay ungraded until the desktop or mobile composition is selected. Each generation record includes the post-processing settings. To reproduce the finish from a source PNG, run `uv run --with pillow --with numpy python scripts/grade-made-photo.py path/to/source.png`; the script writes sibling `-film.png` and `-film.json` files.

All four campaigns also have silent five-second background videos, generated with `kwaivgi/kling-v3.0-pro` through OpenRouter from the approved ungraded landscape and portrait photographs. Each request uses the same photograph as its first and last frame. The clips retain small natural pose changes and are not perfectly seamless loops. Their prompts and processing records are included in each product's generation record above. To reproduce the video finish, run `uv run --with imageio-ffmpeg --with pillow --with numpy python scripts/grade-made-video.py path/to/clip-raw.mp4`.

Each product introduction chooses the matching orientation before requesting a video and loops it inline without audio. Visitors can pause it; hidden tabs and offscreen campaigns pause playback, and reduced motion or a playback error keeps the original photograph. The Made index retains the rounded material artwork cards.

Carrel uses a cobalt ceramic squared arch in a library. The corrected campaign casts an original young adult woman with long black hair, wispy bangs and a white blouse, following the selected Japanese/Korean fashion-editorial reference. The original bob haircut, gray office shirt and mature corporate-portrait direction was rejected. Landscape and portrait micro-motion were regenerated from the corrected photographs. Four full-screen chapter photographs alternate with separate content sections; every chapter has a portrait composition. Operational labels remain accessible HTML. The eleven images, two current clips, prompts, hashes, processing settings, two rejected portrait attempts and usage are recorded in [carrel-generation.json](./carrel-generation.json). The correction's image requests report US$0.101483 in OpenRouter cost and US$0.9197685 in upstream inference cost; these fields are not independent charges to add together. The two new video jobs report US$1.12. Initial-release usage is retained separately.
